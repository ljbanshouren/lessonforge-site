Questions teachers ask

Is the content accurate?

Every generation includes the sources and era facts it used, and flags anything you should double-check against your textbook. You stay the expert; we do the typing.

Will it sound like AI wrote it?

The Forge voice is built from real classroom scripts — spoken rhythm, pauses for questions, no "delve", no "in conclusion". Pro lets you tune it to your own tone.

Which curricula does it know?

It adapts to any Grade 1–9 topic you type and mirrors the structure of national curricula. Paste your own syllabus line and it matches that too.

Do students use it?

No. LessonForge is a teacher-only workspace. Nothing is shared with students except what you print or project.
